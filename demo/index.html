canvas-effects

Two animated, ordered-dithered greyscale backgrounds for a 2D canvas. Both of them are behind this text right now, which is the point: they modulate the page colour rather than becoming a picture, so body copy stays readable on top.

Drag anywhere with the mouse held down to stir the smoke. Every ten seconds or so a jet fires in from one edge — about half of them dark, carving a clear channel rather than painting a bright one.

The controls are live. amplitude is the readability dial; wind it up and watch this paragraph get harder to read. levels is the palette size — drop it to two and the dither is doing all the work that is left.

Everything here is CPU-side: a 2D context, typed arrays and putImageData. There is no WebGL and no shader. What makes it cheap is that the expensive field is computed coarse and interpolated up.
